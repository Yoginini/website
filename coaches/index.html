The coach already knows where you are stuck.

  1. Send your practice for review

    Choose which sessions, poses or your full history to share with a coach. They review the angles the app recorded, where your form drifted, and reply with notes, with or without a live call. Sharing is off by default and you can stop it any time.

  2. The call starts already informed

    If you’ve shared, the coach arrives knowing your poses, the cues you keep hearing, and your streak. No warm-up chat about what you’re working on.

  3. Pay per session, in the app or on the web

    Rates are hourly and set by the coach. Payments run through Stripe; the coach keeps 80%. Repeat sessions with the same coach cost the coach less in fees, so long relationships are rewarded.

  4. Video, scheduling and notes in one place

    Book from the coach’s calendar, join the call from the app, and get their notes and a custom sequence afterwards to practise with the AI coach until next time.

Economics

Pay the teacher, not the app.

Of every session, 80% goes to the coach and 20% keeps the calls, calendar, payments and payouts running. Book a coach you have worked with before and our share drops, so the people who stick with you cost less to keep.
