How it works

Three minutes to your first correction.

No wearables, no special mat. The phone you already own, propped against a water bottle, is enough.

  1. READ · 40 SEC 01

    Learn the shape

    A short explanation of the pose, what it should feel like, and the one or two things people usually get wrong.

  2. SETUP · ONCE 02

    Prop up your phone

    We walk you through distance, height and angle the first time. Most bad sessions are framing problems, so we fix framing first.

  3. PRACTICE · 5–60 MIN 03

    Hold, and listen

    The camera tracks 33 points on your body and compares your joint angles to the reference. When something drifts, a calm voice says so.

Privacy, by construction

Your bedroom stays your bedroom.

Every frame is analysed on your phone and thrown away. What leaves the device is a handful of numbers: 33 body points, the angles between them, and your score. There is no video to leak because there is no video to store.

We compare angles rather than positions, so it does not matter how tall you are or how far away you stand. It matters how you hold the shape.

The voice

It knows when to say nothing.

One cue at a time. Never off a single frame. Never twice in five seconds. If three things are off, you hear the one that keeps you safe, and the rest can wait.

Every phrase was written with a teacher, then rotated so it never sounds like a machine reading a list. After you finish, a short summary tells you what went well and what to watch next time.

Things people ask.

Does anyone see my camera feed?+

No. Video is processed on your phone and discarded frame by frame. Our servers receive joint angles and scores, never images. There is nothing to watch, even for us.

I’m a complete beginner. Will it just tell me I’m wrong all the time?+

Every pose ships with beginner, intermediate and advanced tolerances, and over time the app scales to your own baseline. A tight hamstring is a starting point, not an error.

What happens after the $3 trial?+

After 14 days it becomes the $15 monthly membership. We email you two days before, and cancelling takes one tap.

Are coaching sessions included in the membership?+

No. Coaches set their own hourly rate, and you pay per session. Your membership gives you their published poses and sequences to practise between calls.

Can a teacher look at my progress?+

If you want them to. You choose which sessions, poses or your full history to send a coach for review. They see the same angles and scores the app saw, never video, and can reply with notes or a sequence to work on. Sharing is off by default and you can revoke it any time.

Do I need special equipment?+

A phone with a camera and somewhere to lean it. Landscape, about two metres away, roughly hip height. The app checks your framing before each session.

Which phones?+

iPhone first, Android shortly after. Join the waitlist and tell us which you use.

Is Yoginini available yet?+

Not yet. Nothing has shipped, no coaches are signed, and there is no app to download. The waitlist is the only thing that is real today.
